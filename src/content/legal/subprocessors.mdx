## What this list is

A subprocessor is a company Direwolf uses to process customer data on its behalf in order to deliver the Direwolf Platform and services. Each one is bound by a contract that limits its use of the data and requires appropriate security, which is how we meet Colorado's requirement to flow reasonable-security obligations down to service providers (C.R.S. 6-1-713.5). This list is incorporated into our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms/) and described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/).

This list describes the subprocessors Direwolf will use when the Platform launches. The agreement with the endpoint protection vendor is being put in place; see the [Compliance](/compliance/) page for the current status of our program.

## Platform subprocessors

The Platform is being brought into service; these statements describe how customer data is handled once you use it.

| **SentinelOne, Inc.**                             | Endpoint protection platform whose licenses Direwolf resells. Direwolf provisions a dedicated SentinelOne site for your organization and reads alerts, agent inventory and process timelines from it; the Direwolf AI SOC sends response actions (kill, quarantine, isolate, reconnect) to it | Security data from your endpoints: alerts and detections, hostnames and device identifiers, operating system and software details, process names and command lines, file names and hashes, network addresses, signed-in usernames. SentinelOne has two roles. It is Direwolf's subprocessor for the data Direwolf's connectors read from and write to its cloud, and it is your direct licensor under its own end user license agreement, which you accept when you buy its product through the Platform. Its own privacy terms govern its use of the data your endpoints send it, including any de-identified "system data" or "usage data" its terms permit it to keep.

## What is not on this list

Direwolf does not use analytics, advertising, marketing-automation, customer-support chat or third-party AI services that would receive customer or visitor data. If we add a provider in one of these categories, it will appear here first.

## Changes to this list

When we intend to add or replace a subprocessor that will process customer data, we will update this page with a new version and email the administrators of every customer organization at least 30 days before the new subprocessor begins processing, except where a change is needed urgently to address a security or legal risk, in which case we will notify you as soon as we reasonably can. If you have a reasonable, security-based objection to a new subprocessor, you may tell us within that period; if we cannot address the objection, you may terminate the affected subscription before the change takes effect and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused remainder of the term.
