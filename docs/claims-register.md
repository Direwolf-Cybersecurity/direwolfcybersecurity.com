# Claims register

Every factual, security or compliance claim on www.direwolfcybersecurity.com must appear
here with its approved wording, status and evidence (CLAUDE.md, "Accuracy of claims";
platform PLAN.md §14.3). The research behind each row is in `docs/claims-research.md`
(section numbers cited as "research §n"). A page may use a row's wording verbatim or a
shorter paraphrase that claims less, never more. When a row's status changes, edit the
row first, then the page, and bump the Compliance page's "Last updated" date.

**Register version:** v2026.10.24 · **Owner:** Mitch (platform owner) · **Attorney
review:** pending; the site launched on 2026-10-23 at the owner's direction before it (gate G1,
open, tracked in `docs/launch-runbook.md`, "Launch record").

`PLATFORM` below means `/c/Users/mitch/code/direwolf-platform`.

## Wording rules

1. **Never** "certified", "authorized", "accredited", "compliant", "approved" or
   "validated" about Direwolf, the platform or the website unless a row below says
   `true today` for that exact phrase. Today only row F1 does ("SBA certified", about the
   company's SBA VetCert status); no row does for the platform or the website.
2. **FedRAMP** appears only on the Compliance and Platform pages (never in headings, hero
   copy, taglines, titles, meta descriptions, images or badges), is always attributed to
   AWS, is always spelled "FedRAMP", and is always followed by the disclaimer in row H2.
   No FedRAMP logo, ever (research §2.2).
3. **FIPS**: "FIPS 140-validated cryptography" only for TLS termination at the load
   balancer and AWS KMS, with the certificate numbers on the Compliance page and the short
   forms in rows H4–H5 elsewhere; never "FIPS 140-validated encryption", never for the
   platform as a whole or for application-level cryptography (research §5).
4. **NIST / CMMC**: "aligned to NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2" and "pursuing / preparing for
   CMMC Level 2 certification" until a C3PAO assessment is complete and a CMMC Status is
   recorded in SPRS. No CMMC or Cyber AB logos (The Cyber AB appears by name only, in the
   Experience section, row E3); no "RPO", "ready", "aligned to CMMC"
   (research §3). The term "C3PAO" appears on the site only inside row C2's Compliance-page
   sentence, where it names the suspended assessment type; everywhere else write "an
   authorized assessor" or "a CMMC assessment", and never describe Direwolf as, or as
   working with, an RPO or C3PAO.
5. **Vendors**: SentinelOne is not named on Home or Services, and never with
   logos, "partner", "authorized", "available through Direwolf", "licensed through
   Direwolf" or "resells", until signed agreements exist (rows V1–V2, research §6). Until
   then the Platform page, `src/data/pricing.ts`, the `/services/#…` anchors and the
   Accessibility Statement are vendor-neutral too (rows A4–A5); vendor names appear only as
   nominative references in the legal pages and the Security page's out-of-scope list
   (row L2). After signing, follow the vendor trademark license. NinjaOne (endpoint
   management, row V2) is retired with the endpoint management service (owner direction
   2026-10-24) and is named nowhere on the site: the legal documents (v2026.10.24.2) and the
   Security page (policy v2026.10.24.2) dropped it on 2026-10-24 (row L2).
6. **People, customers, numbers**: no customer names, logos, testimonials, counts,
   percentages, response times, headcount, years of experience, certifications held by
   staff, or founder biographies unless a row is added with evidence. No invented
   quotations. The Experience section is governed by rows E1-E6: organization names in text
   under the lead sentence "Our team has supported programs and organizations including:",
   each shown only once its row records the owner's confirmation (gate G18), never called
   clients, customers or partners, never "trusted by", and logos only with the mark owner's
   written permission (`docs/logo-use-research.md`).
7. **Company facts**: ownership is stated as "Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small
   Business (SDVOSB), SBA certified" (row F1: that exact phrase, its noun-phrase form "an
   SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business", or the shorter
   "veteran-owned" or "service-disabled veteran-owned"); never "VA-verified" or "CVE-verified",
   never "VOSB" alone or spelled out ("veteran-owned small business" understates the status), and never
   the SBA or VetCert icons unless SBA's icon rules are followed (`docs/launch-runbook.md`,
   "Logo use"). "Equal opportunity employer" only (no "affirmative action employer", no
   M/F/D/V tagline). "Headquartered in Colorado Springs, Colorado" with the business
   mailing address (PO box or registered agent) from `src/config.ts` once added (research
   §7, §8).
8. **AI and SOC**: describe what the system does and that it is "monitored by a team of
   security professionals" (owner wording 2026-10-24, row A2; for example "AI-driven detection
   and response, monitored by a team of security professionals"). Never "human analyst
   reviewing", "human analyst review" or "a Direwolf analyst reviews" on any page (the legal
   documents use the row A2 wording from v2026.10.24.2), never claim 24/7 human staffing
   ("around the clock" describes only the automated triage, row A1), and never "24x7
   analysts", "guaranteed", "stops all threats", "zero false positives", "replaces your
   security team", or metrics (research §9).
9. **Roadmap**: every item in row R1 is labelled "Roadmap" or "Coming later" wherever it
   appears, with no price and no "available", "included" or "today".
10. **No SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI, HIPAA, ITAR, DoD IL4/IL5, StateRAMP, CJIS or "FedRAMP
    equivalent" claims** about Direwolf (PLAN.md §14.3; research §2.3, §10).
11. **Legal pages** are "drafts for attorney review" internally; the site shows only
    "Last updated vYYYY.MM.DD". Do not say "attorney-reviewed" until it is true.
12. **Website versus platform**: the website is on GitHub Pages, outside the platform
    boundary; nothing may imply the website shares the platform's hosting or controls
    (row W1).
13. **Third-party statistics**: only the figures in rows S-STAT-1 to S-STAT-6, in their
    approved wording or a paraphrase that claims less, each with its source (publisher,
    publication and year) shown beside it. Never present a third-party figure as Direwolf's
    own result, never imply Direwolf prevents the outcome or delivers the saving, never drop
    a qualifier (for example "in the most extreme cases"), and never combine figures from
    different sources in one sentence. Research and citations: `docs/threat-stats.md`.
