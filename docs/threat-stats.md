# Threat statistics for the website

Third-party statistics about the threats small and mid-size businesses face, for the Home
statistics band and supporting copy. Each one was opened at the publisher on 2026-10-24 and is
registered in `docs/claims-register.md` as rows S-STAT-1 to S-STAT-6 with status `true today
(third-party statistic, cited)`. Use a figure only with the wording approved in its row, and
always show the publication and year next to it.

All six come from the most recent edition available on 2026-10-24:

| Row      | Figure             | Publication                                                 | Published  |
| -------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| S-STAT-1 | USD 1.93 million   | IBM, Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026                      | 2026-07-29 |
| S-STAT-2 | 48%                | Verizon, 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report (19th ed.)  | 2026-05-19 |
| S-STAT-3 | more than 7%       | Verizon, 2026 Breach Impact Study (first edition)           | 2026-06-16 |
| S-STAT-4 | 34% (versus 46%)   | Sophos, The State of Ransomware 2026 (7th ed.)              | July 2026  |
| S-STAT-5 | half (26% and 24%) | Sophos, The State of Ransomware 2026 (7th ed.)              | July 2026  |
| S-STAT-6 | USD 3.05 billion   | FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, 2025 IC3 Annual Report | April 2026 |

Recommended for a four-figure Home statistics band: S-STAT-3 (SMB impact), S-STAT-4 (small
organizations stop fewer attacks), S-STAT-2 (ransomware) and S-STAT-1 (security AI and
automation). S-STAT-5 and S-STAT-6 fit the penetration testing service (phishing simulation).

## S-STAT-1: Security AI and automation lowers the cost of a breach

- **Figure:** USD 1.93 million lower average breach cost for organizations that make extensive
  use of security AI and automation, compared with organizations that use none. Companion
  figure on the same page: the global average cost of a data breach was a record USD 4.99
  million, up 12% on the previous year.
- **Supports:** "Organizations that make extensive use of security AI and automation saw
  breach costs USD 1.93 million lower than those that use none (IBM, Cost of a Data Breach
  Report 2026)." Companion: "The global average cost of a data breach reached a record USD
  4.99 million (IBM, Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026)."
- **Quote:** "Cost savings, in USD, from extensive use of AI and automation in security"
- **Publication:** IBM, _Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026_, conducted by Ponemon Institute,
  sponsored and analyzed by IBM; 602 organizations, breaches between March 2025 and February 2026. Released 2026-07-29.
- **Where:** report landing page, first section ("The AI tipping point"), headline figures
  "4.99M" and "1.93M". Confirmed in IBM's press release of 2026-07-29 and the IBM X-Force
  article of the same date, which gives the same USD 1.93 million per breach. The full
  report PDF sits behind a registration form and was not opened, so no PDF page is cited.
- **URLs:** <https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach>;
  <https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-29-ibm-study-one-in-four-malicious-breaches-are-ai-enabled,-costing-companies-6-million-on-average>;
  <https://www.ibm.com/think/x-force/2026-cost-of-a-data-breach-ai-adversaries-enterprise-risk>
- **Caveats:** a global average across organizations of every size, not an SMB figure; it
  compares organizations, it does not measure what any one product saves. Never present it as
  a saving Direwolf delivers.

## S-STAT-2: Ransomware is in almost half of all breaches

- **Figure:** ransomware was present in 48% of breaches, up from 44% the year before. On the
  same page: 69% of ransomware victims did not pay, and the median ransom paid was USD
  139,875. The report's SMB section adds, without a figure, that small organizations are
  disproportionately affected by ransomware.
- **Supports:** "Ransomware was involved in 48% of breaches (Verizon, 2026 Data Breach
  Investigations Report)." Optional second sentence: "Verizon reports that small
  organizations are disproportionately affected by ransomware."
- **Quote:** "Ransomware grew again to 48% of all breaches"
- **Publication:** Verizon, _2026 Data Breach Investigations Report_ (19th edition),
  Executive Summary. More than 31,000 incidents, of which more than 22,000 were confirmed
  breaches, in 145 countries; incidents from October 2024 to November 2025. Published 2026-05-19.
- **Where:** Executive Summary, page 10, "Growth in ransomware and third-party breaches
  continues"; SMB sentence on page 16, "Small- and medium-sized businesses".
- **URLs:** <https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/executivebriefs/2026-dbir-executive-summary.pdf>;
  landing page <https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/reports/dbir/>
- **Caveats:** the 48% is across all organizations. The full report (where an SMB-specific
  ransomware share would be) is behind a form and was not opened, so no SMB percentage from
  the 2026 DBIR is registered.

## S-STAT-3: The worst breaches cost small businesses more than 7% of revenue

- **Figure:** for small and medium-sized businesses (under USD 25 million revenue), economic
  impact was as high as 3% of revenue in the top 10% of cyber insurance claims and over 7% in
  the most extreme 2.5%. Mid-market and large organizations stayed under 2% even in their top
  2.5%. The median SMB impact was about USD 38,000, and ransomware made up 39% of SMB claims.
- **Supports:** "In the most extreme cases, a breach cost a small or medium-sized business
  more than 7% of its annual revenue (Verizon, 2026 Breach Impact Study)."
- **Quote:** "over 7% in the more extreme top 2.5% of cases"
- **Publication:** Verizon, _2026 Breach Impact Study_, the first edition, by the authors of
  the DBIR. About 70,000 U.S. cyber insurance claims (about 38,000 with paid losses) for
  incidents from 2019-01-01 to 2025-10-31; SMB means under USD 25 million revenue. Published
  2026-06-16 (date from Verizon's SMB infographic).
- **Where:** page 8, "Key findings"; page 29, "Small- and medium-sized businesses" (which
  phrases it as "up to 7%"; ransomware 39%, business email compromise 19%). Verizon's SMB
  infographic shows it as ">7%".
- **URLs:** <https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/reports/2026-breach-impact-study-dbir.pdf>;
  infographic <https://www.verizon.com/business/resources/infographics/2026-dbir-bis-smb-revenue-losses.pdf>
- **Caveats:** a tail figure from insured U.S. claims, not a typical outcome. Always keep "in
  the most extreme cases" (or "top 2.5% of claims"); never "a breach costs SMBs 7% of
  revenue".

## S-STAT-4: Small organizations stop fewer ransomware attacks in time

- **Figure:** among organizations hit by ransomware, 34% of the smallest surveyed (100 to 250
  employees) stopped the attack before data was encrypted or held to ransom, compared with
  46% of the largest (3,001 to 5,000 employees).
- **Supports:** "Only 34% of organizations with 100 to 250 employees stopped a ransomware
  attack before their data was encrypted, against 46% of organizations with 3,001 to 5,000
  employees (Sophos, The State of Ransomware 2026)."
- **Quote:** "compared to just 34% for the smallest (100-250 employees)"
- **Publication:** Sophos, _The State of Ransomware 2026_ (7th annual edition). Independent
  survey by Vanson Bourne for Sophos, January to March 2026: 2,158 IT and security leaders in
  17 countries at organizations of 100 to 5,000 employees, all hit by ransomware in the
  previous 12 months. Published July 2026.
- **Where:** printed page 13 (PDF page 14), "Data encryption and recovery".
- **URLs:** <https://assets.sophos.com/X24WTUEQ/at/jbww7pmb8n3gp99wr6hfq4/sophos-state-ransomware-report-2026.pdf>;
  summary <https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/sophos-state-of-ransomware-2026>
- **Caveats:** vendor-commissioned survey of victims only; the smallest band is 100 employees,
  so it says nothing about businesses under 100.

## S-STAT-5: Email and phishing start half of ransomware attacks

- **Figure:** malicious email (26%) and phishing (24%) were the top two root causes of
  ransomware attacks, together half of all incidents; exploited vulnerabilities fell to 18%.
- **Supports:** "Malicious email and phishing were the root cause of half of ransomware
  attacks (Sophos, The State of Ransomware 2026)."
- **Quote:** "accounting for half of all incidents"
- **Publication:** Sophos, _The State of Ransomware 2026_ (as S-STAT-4).
- **Where:** printed page 3 (PDF page 4), "Attack and defense methods: Root causes of
  attacks"; also in the introduction, printed page 1.
- **URL:** <https://assets.sophos.com/X24WTUEQ/at/jbww7pmb8n3gp99wr6hfq4/sophos-state-ransomware-report-2026.pdf>
- **Caveats:** Verizon's 2026 DBIR, on a different dataset, ranks vulnerability exploitation
  first (31% of breaches) with phishing at 16%; do not combine the two sources in one
  sentence.

## S-STAT-6: Business email compromise cost victims over USD 3 billion

- **Figure:** USD 3,046,598,558 in reported business email compromise (BEC) losses in 2025,
  the second-largest loss category after investment fraud. All 2025 complaints: 1,008,597,
  with USD 20.877 billion in reported losses, up 26% on 2024.
- **Supports:** "Victims reported more than USD 3 billion in business email compromise losses
  to the FBI in 2025 (FBI IC3, 2025 Internet Crime Report)."
- **Quote:** "followed by business email compromises and tech support scams" (FBI, page 3; a
  U.S. government work)
- **Publication:** Federal Bureau of Investigation, Internet Crime Complaint Center, _2025 IC3
  Annual Report_ (the annual Internet Crime Report). PDF dated 2026-04-16.
- **Where:** page 3, "A Quarter-Century of Cybercrime Reporting" (ranking); page 6, "IC3
  Complaints in 2025" (totals); page 26, "Three Year Complaint Loss Comparison" (BEC row).
- **URLs:** <https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf>; index
  <https://www.ic3.gov/annualreport/reports>
- **Caveats:** losses reported to the IC3 by individuals and organizations, not only
  businesses; a floor, since many victims never report.
